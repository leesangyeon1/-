04National Guard × student — verified

SoFi terms — confirmed

No application fee, no origination fee, no prepayment penalty; fixed means fixed for life; $5,000 minimum; serviced by MOHELA; 0.25% autopay discount (plus 0.125% with SoFi Plus direct deposit). Your Sallie Mae loans are already private, so this refinance forfeits no federal benefits.

SCRA 6% cap — the one trade-off

Debt taken before entering service is capped at 6% during qualifying active duty (Title 10, or Title 32 §502(f) >30 days) — private loans included, excess interest refunded. A refinance is a new loan made during service, so it loses SCRA eligibility. Your new rates are ≤6.01% anyway, so the loss is tiny — but if you're on qualifying orders right now, invoke SCRA on the old 9.7–11.25% loans first and refinance after demobilizing. JAG can help file it.

Guard SLRP — won't cover these loans

The Student Loan Repayment Program (up to $50k for critical-skill 6/8-yr contracts, ≈$8,333/yr paid to the lender) applies to qualifying federal loans. Private Sallie Mae/SoFi debt is generally ineligible. If you extend or re-enlist, ask the education & incentives office before signing — but don't delay this refinance waiting on it.

Oklahoma tuition waiver — borrow nothing new

The Oklahoma National Guard Educational Assistance Program waives resident tuition and mandatory fees at Oklahoma public colleges — up to 18 credit hrs/semester (2.0 GPA, satisfactory participation). Stack Federal Tuition Assistance and MGIB-SR (Ch. 1606). Your best "better option" is making this $38,599 the last student debt you ever take.
